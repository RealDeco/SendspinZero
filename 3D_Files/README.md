## 3D Printable Files

3D printing files for the **Sendspin Zero** family of devices. Each project has a `.3mf` (ready to open in Bambu Studio / other slicers) and individual `.stl` files for every part.

---

### Sendspin Zero – Toslink, Analog & Coax

The three main versions. Each one has a front for **with display**, **without display**, or **with a big button**.

| 3MF | Version | STL parts |
|-----|-----|-----|
| `SendspinZero-Toslink.3mf` | Optical Toslink | `SendspinZero-Toslink-back.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Toslink-front-display.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Toslink-front-nodisplay.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Toslink-front-button.stl` |
| `SendspinZero-Analog.3mf` | Analog stereo (3.5 mm minijack) | `SendspinZero-Analog-back.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Analog-front-display.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Analog-front-nodisplay.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Analog-front-button.stl` |
| `SendspinZero-Coax.3mf` | Coax SPDIF | `SendspinZero-Coax-back.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Coax-front-display.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Coax-front-nodisplay.stl`<br>`SendspinZero-Coax-front-button.stl` |

**Notes**

- Printed in PETG and not tested in PLA, size "could" vary. (feedback welcome)
- The `.3mf` files were created with an **older version of Bambu Studio** to ensure broad compatibility.
- No 3D files (yet) for the Speaker amplifier version or SendspinZero-Ear.
